Overview

The Challenge

An investor presentation is the story management wants investors to believe, told with carefully chosen numbers. Separating the real drivers of value from the sales pitch means reading it alongside an independent source and knowing which signals matter for the kind of company it is.

The Solution

The AI Strategy Analysis Agent reads the investor presentation and the credit rating report side by side. It first decides what kind of company this is, then looks for the signals that matter for that type, and writes a memo that sets the pitch against the reality check. If a presentation is too large to analyse in one pass, it reads it in sections and combines the findings.

Investment Memo Framework

  • Narrative Diagnosis: Classifies the company as a Compounder, Aggressor, Turnaround or Special Situation
  • The Sales Pitch: Summarises the story management is selling and the metrics it highlights most
  • The Reality Check: Uses the credit report to test the pitch: margin mix, capex efficiency and hidden assets
  • Sector KPIs: Extracts the 3-5 operating numbers that define the industry's health, such as NIM for banks or order book for capital goods
  • Roadmap and Verdict: The single biggest change expected in the next 3 years, with a bull case and a bear case

Key Differentiators

  • Pitch vs Reality: Grounds management's claims in an independent credit rating report
  • Category-Aware Lens: Looks for different evidence depending on the type of company
  • Handles Large Decks: Automatically switches to section-by-section (map-reduce) analysis when a presentation is too large
  • Feeds Other Agents: Its sector KPIs are passed to the Valuation and Qualitative agents

System Architecture

The Strategy Agent follows a two-step workflow: the downloader fetches the investor presentation and credit report into memory, and the agent reads both to produce the investment memo.
