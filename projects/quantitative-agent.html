AI Quantitative Analysis Agent

This Agent performs Quantitative analysis for a given stock using the 10-year Excel sheet downloaded from screener.in, with charts and a written assessment

Launch Application Learn More

Overview

The Challenge

Ten years of profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flow statements hold the real story of a company, but reading them line by line takes time. The most important question is easy to miss: is the profit the company reports actually turning into cash?

The Solution

The AI Quantitative Analysis Agent downloads the company's financial data sheet from Screener.in, parses the three statements, calculates operating profit margins itself, and asks Gemini for a structured, data-driven assessment. The result is a report that combines six charts with written analysis, positive highlights and red flags.

Analysis Framework

  • Revenue and Profitability: Year-on-year sales and net profit growth, and the operating profit margin trend
  • Balance Sheet: Borrowings trend and growth in reserves
  • Cash Flow Quality: Cash from operations compared with net profit, year by year and cumulatively
  • Overall Summary: Key findings, 2-3 positive highlights and 2-3 red flags to monitor

Key Differentiators

  • Visual Report: Six charts: sales vs profit, margin trend, borrowings, reserves, operating cash flow, and cash flow vs profit
  • Independent Margin Calculation: Operating margin is computed from the raw expense lines rather than taken on trust
  • Feeds Other Agents: Its summary is reused by the Valuation and Synthesis agents as verified financial context
  • Privacy-First Architecture: The spreadsheet and charts are processed entirely in memory (io.BytesIO)

System Architecture

The Quantitative Agent follows a simple two-step workflow: the downloader fetches the Excel data sheet into memory, and the agent turns it into charts and analysis for the dashboard.
