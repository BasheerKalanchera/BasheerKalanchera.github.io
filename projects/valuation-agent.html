AI Valuation Analysis Agent

This agent does sector-aware peer valuation of a stock based on data retrieved from screener.in, and tells you whether it looks undervalued, fairly valued or overvalued

Overview

The Challenge

A P/E of 40 can be cheap for one company and expensive for another. Judging value means comparing a company with its closest rivals, using the measures that matter for its industry: net interest margin for a bank, revenue per bed for a hospital, order book for a capital goods maker. Done by hand, this is slow, and generic AI tools tend to fill gaps with numbers they have made up.

The Solution

The AI Valuation Analysis Agent pulls the peer comparison table from Screener.in, runs the Quantitative and Strategy agents first to gather verified financial ratios and industry KPIs, and then loads a valuation rulebook written for the company's sector. It compares the company with its 3-5 closest peers, checks governance, and gives a clear verdict, citing only numbers that appear in the data it was given.

Valuation Framework

  • Sector Rulebook: Loads one of 11 sector-specific valuation methodologies (banking, pharma, IT, FMCG, hospitals, auto and more), with a general fallback
  • Peer Selection: Identifies the target company in the peer table and selects its 3-5 closest competitors
  • Sector Metric Check: Evaluates every metric the sector rulebook asks for against its benchmark, with a short assessment for each
  • Governance Check: Flags promoter share pledging as a red flag
  • Valuation Verdict: Compares the company with the peer average and classifies it as Undervalued, Fairly Valued or Overvalued

Key Differentiators

  • Grounded Output: Uses only values present in the supplied data; missing metrics are marked "Data Not Available" instead of guessed
  • Cross-Agent Context: Builds on the Quantitative Agent's pre-computed ratios and the Strategy Agent's sector KPIs
  • Pluggable Sector Skills: Valuation rules live in editable Markdown files, so a new sector can be added without code changes
  • Resilient Execution: Automatic retries and model fallback when API rate limits are hit

System Architecture

The Valuation Agent runs last in its workflow. Data is fetched once, the Quantitative and Strategy agents build the context, and the Valuation Agent applies the sector rulebook to produce its verdict.
