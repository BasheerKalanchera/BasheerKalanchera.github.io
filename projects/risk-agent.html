AI Risk Analysis Agent

This agent performs risk analysis using the company's credit rating report and produces a structured risk profile

Launch Application Learn More

Overview

The Challenge

Credit rating agency reports are one of the best independent views of a company's financial health, but they are long, technical, and written in different formats by different agencies. An old report can also be mistaken for a current one.

The Solution

The AI Risk Analysis Agent fetches the company's latest available credit rating report, checks that the file is valid, and extracts its text. Acting as a sceptical credit analyst, it produces a structured risk profile that focuses on whether the company can survive a downturn, and it states the date of the report so the reader knows how current the analysis is.

Risk Profile Framework

  • Company Overview: What the company does, with the date of the report being analysed
  • Credit Rating and Outlook: The long-term rating and outlook, for example AA+ / Stable
  • Key Strengths: Competitive advantages and market position
  • Structural Risks: Promoter issues, working capital traps and exposure to industry cycles
  • Liquidity and Debt: Cash reserves, debt levels, interest coverage and key debt ratios

Key Differentiators

  • Multi-Agency Support: Works with reports from CRISIL, ICRA, CARE, India Ratings and other agencies, in PDF or HTML
  • Report Date Transparency: Every profile states the date of the underlying report
  • Input Validation: Detects broken or invalid downloads before analysis, and falls back to an alternative report link when one fails
  • Feeds Other Agents: Its risk profile is reused by the Qualitative and Synthesis agents

System Architecture

The Risk Agent follows a two-step workflow: the downloader fetches the credit rating report into memory, and the agent turns it into a structured risk profile.
