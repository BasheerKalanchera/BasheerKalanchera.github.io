AI Homeschooling Tutor

An adaptive, voice-enabled tutor for CBSE students (Grades 1-12) that teaches from the child's own textbook pages in English, Hindi or Malayalam

Launch Application Learn More

Overview

The Challenge

Homeschooling parents cannot always sit beside their child for every subject. Generic AI chatbots give away answers instead of teaching, drift away from the school syllabus, and are hard for young children to use by typing. Regional-language textbooks, often scanned, add another barrier.

The Solution

The AI Homeschooling Tutor, "Gyan Mitra", reads the child's actual textbook chapter, whether a clean PDF, a scanned page or a photo, and teaches strictly from it. Children can talk to it and hear its replies, in an Indian accent for Hindi and Malayalam. It teaches through guiding questions, reviews key points, runs quizzes, and adapts its explanations as it tracks how well the child is doing.

How It Works

  • Load the Chapter: Upload textbook PDFs or photos, or pick a sample chapter; scanned pages are read with OCR in English, Hindi or Malayalam
  • Talk or Type: Children can speak their answers or type them; replies are read aloud
  • Three Modes: Learn (guiding questions), Review (summaries and key points) and Quiz
  • Adaptive Difficulty: Tracks right and wrong answers and moves between Struggling, Getting It and Mastered, simplifying when the child struggles
  • Progress Dashboard: Streaks, study time, quiz scores, subject performance and weak areas. The dashboard is built; connecting it to live tutoring sessions is in progress

Key Differentiators

  • Syllabus-Grounded: Every explanation and question is based on the uploaded textbook pages, not general internet knowledge
  • Teaches, Doesn't Tell: Socratic method in Learn mode: one small idea or question at a time, with the direct answer only when the child is truly stuck
  • Multilingual Reading: Detects native versus scanned PDFs and runs OCR with Hindi and Malayalam language packs
  • Made for Listening: Each reply has a display version with proper maths notation and a speech version spelled out for text-to-speech

System Architecture

Textbook pages are read once and turned into lesson context. Each question from the child, spoken or typed, goes to Gyan Mitra together with the current mode and mastery level, and the reply comes back both on screen and as speech.
