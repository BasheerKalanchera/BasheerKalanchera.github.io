AI News Agent

A self-hosted AI agent that turns the last 24 hours of tech news into a short, ranked "Morning AI Executive Summary"

Learn More

Overview

The Challenge

Keeping up with AI means scanning dozens of headlines every day across several sources, and most of them are not about AI at all: general tech, politics, conflicts, markets. Reading everything takes time, and a simple list of links does not tell you what matters most.

The Solution

The AI News Agent runs as an n8n workflow in Docker on a laptop. With one click it collects the latest stories from Techmeme and The Rundown AI, keeps the last 24 hours, and applies free, rule-based filtering to remove off-topic and duplicate stories. Google Gemini then ranks the shortlist by importance, drops the noise, and the agent builds a clean executive briefing with the most important stories first and a direct link to each original article.

How It Works

  • One-Click Trigger: Open the briefing form and press Generate Report
  • Collect: Pulls the latest stories from the Techmeme and The Rundown AI RSS feeds
  • Freshness Filter: Keeps only stories from the last 24 hours and merges both feeds
  • Keyword Shortlist: Keeps AI-related stories, drops off-topic ones such as conflicts, elections and sports, removes duplicates, and caps the list at the 15 newest
  • AI Ranking: Google Gemini classifies each story as High, Medium or Low importance from its headline and opening lines; Low stories are dropped and High stories are shown first
  • Executive Briefing: A clean summary page with each story's headline, a short description and a direct link to the original article

Key Differentiators

  • Cheap Before Smart: Free, instant keyword rules do the bulk filtering, so the AI is only used where judgment is needed
  • Built for Free-Tier Limits: Stories are ranked two at a time with a pause, and only short text is sent, keeping within API rate limits
  • Failure-Tolerant: If the AI service fails on a story, that story is skipped and the briefing is still delivered
  • Self-Hosted and Editable: Runs in Docker on a laptop; the keyword lists and ranking rules are plain text that can be tuned without code changes

Lessons From Building It

  • Filter first: Sending every story to the AI hit rate limits within seconds; a keyword pre-filter cut the AI's workload to a short, relevant list
  • Model choice matters: A reasoning model "thought out loud" instead of giving the strict answer format the ranking step needs; a Flash-Lite model followed the format reliably
  • Send less text: Some feeds include whole newsletters; sending only the headline and opening lines made ranking faster and more reliable
